# AURA Desktop Music Player

A lightweight Windows music player with a clean, modern interface. Search and play music, discover your next favorite, organize playlists, and enjoy offline listening.

## Download

[Download AURA for Windows](https://github.com/NoorAshraful/AURA-Desktop-Music-application/releases/latest/download/Aura-Setup-x64.exe)

Download **Aura-Setup-x64.exe** from the [latest release](https://github.com/NoorAshraful/AURA-Desktop-Music-application/releases/latest), then run the installer.

## Features

- **Discover:** personal artist mixes, saved playlists, recently played music, favorites, and genre exploration.
- **Continuous playback:** playlists finish their selected songs before continuing with related music. Shuffle, Repeat, and Play next keep their intended behavior.
- **Search and play:** find music online, view thumbnails, and start playback with one click.
- **Your collection:** import local audio, paste supported music links, and organize songs into playlists.
- **Offline listening:** download supported tracks and playlists to your PC.
- **Personalization:** light and dark themes, playlist colors, and custom covers.
- **Desktop controls:** a compact mini player, Windows taskbar controls, and the global **Ctrl + Space** play/pause shortcut.
- **Updates:** check for new versions from **Settings > General > Updates**.

Online playback, recommendations, and downloads depend on the source and your internet connection. Memory use varies with your library, playback activity, and system.

## Updating

Aura 0.11.0 and newer can check for updates inside the app. You can also download and run the latest installer. Updating preserves your existing library, playlists, and preferences.

The `aura-update.json` release asset is used by Aura's updater; you do not need to open it manually.
